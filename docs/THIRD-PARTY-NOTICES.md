# Notices, Synthetic Data Boundary & Non-Affiliation

This standalone static showcase presents the frozen **GoSil** precious-metals accounting and statistical research project (final private milestone: D23 locked read-only readiness, `2026-11-16`) as a zero-backend educational portfolio demonstration.

## 1. 100% Synthetic Data Declaration
- Every price, exchange rate, transaction lot, physical bar/coin entry, bid/ask spread, and 36-month forecast evaluation series rendered in this showcase is **100% synthetic** and authored solely for educational demonstration.
- **Zero external market datasets** (no IMF PCPS, no BIS `WS_XRU`, no Borsa İstanbul, no retail exchange feeds) are embedded, cached, or fetched at runtime.
- **Zero real personal financial records** (no real holdings, bank balances, receipts, account identifiers, or private SQLite databases) are included or accessible.
